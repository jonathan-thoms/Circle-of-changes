# Design System & Brand Visual Guidelines

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** `Poppins` (Google Fonts)
- **Weights:** 
  - H1, H2, Hero: Bold (700)
  - H3, Subheaders, Buttons: SemiBold (600)
  - Body, Captions: Regular (400)

## Primary Color Tokens (70-80% usage)
- `--color-deep-ocean`: `#114B5F` (Primary anchor, Headers, Primary Buttons)
- `--color-teal-green`: `#317B78` (Accent highlights, visual elements)
- `--color-sky-renewal`: `#A3D9FF` (Soft card backgrounds, tag badges)
- `--color-sand-beige`: `#EDE4D4` (Warm neutral section backgrounds)
- `--color-charcoal-ink`: `#1F1F1F` (Body text copy)

## Secondary Color Tokens (20-30% usage)
- `--color-impact-green`: `#1A7F5A` (CTA focus, active indicators)
- `--color-regen-mint`: `#9FE7C1` (Light badge highlights)
- `--color-recycled-navy`: `#123154` (Dark mode backgrounds, footers)
- `--color-lavender-gray`: `#D2D7E3` (Muted borders, inactive states)
- `--color-eco-cream`: `#F3F1EC` (Clean page backgrounds)

## Logo Component Rules
- Full horizontal logo used in main Navbar on light backgrounds (`#114B5F` + `#317B78`).
- White logo version used on dark backgrounds (`#114B5F` or `#123154`).
- Sub-branded logo ("Sammie's Circles of Change") used exclusively on the Sammie's Buddy Bench initiative landing page.
- Do NOT place full-color logo on Teal Green or Mint backgrounds due to contrast rules.
